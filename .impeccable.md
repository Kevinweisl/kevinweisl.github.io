# Design Context

## Target Audience
Academic peers, potential collaborators, graduate students, and the ccClub community (4,000+ learners). Visitors are primarily researchers in NLP/IR, faculty, and industry professionals exploring collaboration.

## Use Cases
- Learn about Kevin's research and publications
- Find contact information for collaboration
- Read Kevin's notes/articles on research and community building
- Understand Kevin's background (academia + 6 years industry)

## Brand Personality / Tone
- **Professional but approachable**: Academic rigor without being cold or intimidating
- **Knowledgeable but warm**: Bridges research and real-world applications
- **Distinctive but not flashy**: Memorable without being distracting from content

## Existing Brand Colors

Sampled from the ccClub logo asset itself, with each colour's share of the mark —
the share is what tells you whether a colour is a lead or a garnish, so record it:

- Ice blue `#CADFE3` — 72.7%, the field the mark sits on
- Teal `#487981` — 9.1%
- Pink `#E288B3` — 7.0%

Teal and pink are near-equal in area, so pink is a **second brand colour, not an
accent**. Never record brand colours from memory: this file previously named the
second colour as Coral Red `#D24E42`, which does not appear in the logo at all,
and that error kept pink out of the site for a full redesign cycle.

- Current site: dark only. A deep teal ground (`#0A1519`) answers the logo's
  ice blue, which survives as the primary CTA fill. Chosen by Kevin from four
  side-by-side options — see `tasks/palette-options.html` — then reduced to
  the dark half, then revised per `tasks/design-review/`.
- Raw `#E288B3` fails AA as text. The text pink keeps the logo's hue and
  saturation and moves only lightness to reach 4.5:1 — `#EAA9C8` (`--brand`).

### Colour roles (2026-09-05)

One colour, one job. Teal `--accent` `#7FB6C0` means **clickable** — links,
buttons, hover and focus states — and nothing else. Pink `--brand` `#EAA9C8`
has exactly three jobs: the author's own name in a byline, the emphasised word
in a section title, and an in-progress marker (`Present`). Labels (category
names, venue badges, the hero's tag heading) use the `.label` utility — 12px,
600, uppercase, tracked, `--text-muted` — and are told apart by case and
tracking so colour stays free for interaction. Non-clickable text never wears
`--accent`.

Surfaces: one ground, no alternating section backgrounds (the pair this
replaced were 1.09 apart). Sections separate with a 1px `--border` hairline.
`--border` is `#2B4D57` — 1.76 against a card, 2.03 against the page — bright
enough to define an edge without lifting the card itself.

## Constraints
- Dark only — one theme, no toggle, no `prefers-color-scheme`
- Static export (GitHub Pages), Next.js + Tailwind CSS
- Serif headings (Young Serif) + Sans body (Albert Sans) with Noto Sans/Serif TC for CJK
- Young Serif ships weight 400 only and `font-synthesis-weight` is `none`, so no
  serif element declares a weight — hierarchy is size and space. Enforced by
  `src/lib/typography.test.ts`
- Content column is 720px (~83 characters at 16px); the hero and nav stay at 1100px
